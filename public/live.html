How this works

The <iframe> above loads a public Gradio Space directly from huggingface.co. Your browser streams the text prompt to HF, HF runs the model on their CPU pool, and the audio streams back. Zero deploy, zero install on your end.

  1. Type a description of the music you want ("80s synthwave with drum machine, 120 BPM"). Be specific — instruments, tempo, mood all help.
  2. Hit Generate. ~15 s of stereo audio comes back. Play it inline, download as WAV.
  3. Want your own copy? Hit "Copy full command" on any card in the Tools tab — paste it once with your HF token and you've got a personal Space in < 2 minutes.

Set your HF token (optional · for deploying your own)

Browsing is token-free. The token is only used if you want to deploy a personal copy of a tool via the gradio deploy command. We never send your token to any third party — it stays in your browser's localStorage.
